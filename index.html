Everything you need to see where money goes.

A focused toolkit for the small financial decisions that add up. No noise, no cloud dashboard, no hidden audience.

Subscriptions

Track weekly to yearly billing cycles, renewal dates, monthly burn rate, and on-device reminders.

Debt ledger

Keep borrowed liabilities and lent assets separate. Record partial settlements and see the balance change.

Daily spending

Organize expenses by category, set monthly thresholds, and spot your rhythm with a 30-day heatmap.

Built differently

Private by default.

Your financial records belong on your device. SubDebt works without an account, remote server, analytics, or telemetry.

  • Offline persistence
  • Biometric lock
  • JSON backup & restore
  • CSV data export
